Curriculum Framework

CBSE & NEP 2020 Integration

Evergreen Public School follows the CBSE curriculum from Play to Class 10th. We have fully adopted the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, shifting from rote learning to a pedagogical structure that focuses on the cognitive development of the child.

  • Foundational: Play to Class 2
  • Preparatory: Class 3 to 5
  • Middle: Class 6 to 8
  • Secondary: Class 9 and 10
